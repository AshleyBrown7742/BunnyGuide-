Your weekly GLP-1 injection

A once-weekly shot just under the skin. We'll walk you through your first one in the office. Use this page as your refresher at home.

Step 1

Draw up your dose

  1. Wash your hands.
  2. Wipe the vial stopper with alcohol. Let it dry.
  3. Pull air into the syringe equal to your dose.
  4. Push the air into the vial. Keep the needle in.
  5. Flip the vial upside down. Draw a little past your dose.
  6. Tap out any bubbles and push back to your exact dose in units, as written on your label.
Step 2

Give your injection

  1. Wipe your spot with alcohol. Let it dry fully. Wet alcohol stings.
  2. Pinch a soft fold of skin.
  3. Insert the needle straight in, at 90 degrees.
  4. Let go of the pinch. Push the plunger slowly.
  5. Pull the needle out and press the swab gently. Don't rub.
  6. Drop the syringe straight into your sharps container.
Good habits

Always measure in units on your syringe. Morning is often best (B12 in some formulas can be energizing). Same day every week. Write the injection date in Heads Up.

Never change your dose on your own

Only your provider can move you to a new level. Losing more than 1.5 pounds a week? It is not safe to increase your dose. We'll keep you where you are.

Storage & travel

  • Keep it in the fridge. Never freeze it.
  • Write the date on the vial the first time you use it. Discard 28 days later, even if some is left.
  • Traveling? It can be out of the fridge up to 4 days. Keep it cool and out of the sun.
  • Cloudy, has particles, or arrived warm? Don't use it. Call us.

Missed a dose?

  1. Next dose more than 4 days away? Take it as soon as you remember.
  2. Next dose within 4 days? Skip it and stay on your normal day.
  3. Never double up.

Missed 2+ weeks? Message us before restarting.

Needle disposal

In Massachusetts, needles can't go in the trash. Choose one:

  1. Order a sharps container from a pharmacy or online.
  2. Bring them to your next visit in a sharps container or a sealed heavy plastic bottle (like a detergent bottle). We'll dispose of them.
  3. Drop them at a local collection site.
GLP-1 Support

Side effect support

Nausea

Take a ginger supplement or sip ginger tea. Smaller meals, eaten slowly. Stop before you feel full. Skip greasy foods.

Constipation

Magnesium glycinate in the evening with plenty of fluids, as directed. Eat 2 prunes or dates daily. Keep up your fiber and water.

Digestion

Better Bitters spray 10 minutes before meals.

Good to know

  • Small redness or a bump where you injected is common and fades in a day or two.
  • On birth control pills? We recommend adding a second form of birth control, like condoms.
  • Pregnant or planning to be? Stop and tell us right away. GLP-1s haven't been studied in pregnancy and may be harmful.
  • Shop our GLP side effects protocol on Fullscript.

Call us if

  • Nausea or vomiting that won't settle, or you can't keep fluids down
  • You think you took the wrong dose
  • A lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, or trouble swallowing
  • Red, hot or painful skin that spreads

Call 911 or go to the ER for severe belly pain that spreads to your back, or swelling of the face or throat, or trouble breathing.

Hormone therapy

How to use your hormones, what to expect, and how we keep you safe. Your plan may use one, two or all three.

Cream · Patch · Vaginal

Estrogen

Progesterone

  • Balances estrogen
  • Protects the uterine lining
  • Deeper, calmer sleep
Cream

Testosterone

  • Libido and desire
  • Energy and motivation
  • Mental clarity and strength

Applying your creams

  • Where: inner thigh, inner arm or abdomen. Rotate spots every day.
  • Clean, dry skin. Let it dry before dressing.
  • Wash your hands right after applying.

Protect others from transfer

  • Keep the area covered with clothing.
  • Apply at least 8 hours before intimacy, or after.
  • Keep children and pets away from treated skin.

Vaginal estrogen

Works best applied after intimacy, so it doesn't transfer to your partner.

Testosterone: vulvar option

If your provider recommends it, it can be applied to the clitoris and labia in the morning or after intimacy. Start 2 days a week here and use your usual spots on other days. Watch for clitoral enlargement. If it happens, stop vulvar use and call us.

Taking progesterone

  • At bedtime. It can make you sleepy.
  • With a little healthy fat, like a spoonful of almond butter, for better absorption.
  • No period for 3+ months? Take it every night.
  • Still having periods? Cycle it, as shown, unless told otherwise.
  • Peanut allergy? Tell us. Some capsules contain peanut oil.

Who needs progesterone?

  • Uterus + estrogen: required, to protect your lining
  • Partial hysterectomy (cervix kept): required
  • Mirena IUD in place: oral progesterone not required
  • Full hysterectomy: not required, but highly recommended

What to expect

Wk 1–2

Your body is adjusting. Changes may be subtle.

Wk 3–4

Early improvements in sleep, energy or mood.

Wk 6–8

Fewer hot flashes, better libido, clearer thinking.

~3 mo

Your plan is fine-tuned based on how you feel and your labs.

Your follow-up plan

  • 4 to 6 weeks after starting or any dose change: labs and a check-in
  • Once stable: labs at least every 6 months
  • Every year: your full initial panel
  • Hormone labs don't require fasting. See blood draw timing for creams.

Staying safe

  • Pelvic ultrasound every 5 years is recommended to check your uterine lining.
  • Keep up with mammograms and routine screenings.
  • Missed a dose? Skip it. Never double up.
  • Never change your dose without talking to us.
  • Testosterone and pregnancy: pregnant, might be, or trying to conceive? Stop testosterone and call us. Not for use while breastfeeding.

Signs your dose may need adjusting

Common, and usually fixed with a small change. Let us know about: bloating or swelling in hands or feet, breast tenderness or spotting, acne or oily skin, hair loss or new hair growth, blood pressure changes, anxiety or trouble sleeping.

Reach out right away for

Bleeding that's new, heavier or after menopause · any breast change · strong or recurring headaches · clitoral enlargement or a deeper voice.

Get emergency care for chest pain or trouble breathing · pain, swelling or warmth in one leg · sudden face drooping, weakness, confusion, or trouble speaking or seeing.

Skin · Hair · Longevity

GHK-Cu: the copper peptide

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring peptide that binds to copper. It may help support the body's natural repair environment and the proteins involved in firmness, texture and follicle health.

Shipped cold

Compounded and delivered to your door.

Keep refrigerated

Temperature-sensitive. Consistency matters more than amount.

8–12 weeks

Typical time to see results.

Skin · Topical serum

How to use

  1. Apply after cleansing, to dry skin.
  2. Wait about 10 minutes before moisturizer.
  3. Rotate every other day with other actives (like retinol).
  4. Use mineral sunscreen daily. Stop if it burns or stings for long in the sun.
Hair · Scalp solution

How to use

  1. Apply to a clean, dry scalp once daily. No rinsing needed.
  2. Part hair in sections for even coverage.
  3. Avoid heat styling right after applying.
  4. Store refrigerated.

What to expect

Skin: smoother, more even texture, better firmness and a softer look of fine lines over 8 to 12 weeks. Hair: fuller look and better scalp density over 8 to 12 weeks, improving over 3 to 6 months.

Purging can happen

A temporary purge, especially if you're acne-prone, can happen as cell turnover speeds up. It typically settles within a few weeks. We track skin results with an Aura Scan before starting and at 8 to 12 weeks.

Stop and contact us

Significant burning, swelling, hives, blistering, or a persistent or worsening rash. Don't use with a known metal or copper allergy or Wilson's disease, or if pregnant or breastfeeding. Topical use only. Avoid your eyes.
