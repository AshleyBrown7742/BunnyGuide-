The Program

About the program

Why start with a Reset instead of medication?

Medication works best on a balanced foundation. The Reset shifts your baseline first. Some patients find they need less medication, or none at all. Others start GLP-1 or hormones with fewer side effects.

Is my program 8 or 16 weeks?

Every program runs 16 weeks with a full recheck at the end. Your Tiny Health gut test score decides how long your intensive Reset phase lasts: 8 weeks if your score is over 80, the full 16 weeks if it's under 80. The price is the same either way, because we don't believe in charging you more for needing more time.

Why test the gut?

Research continues to link gut health to hormones, inflammation, mood and metabolism. Testing at the start and the end shows how your gut responded to the program.

Can I start with hormones or GLP-1?

Every journey starts with the Reset. If hormones or lean body mass are part of your goals, we look at whether you're ready around week 8, based on your labs and symptoms. Hormone therapy is offered to women only.

Are visits in person or virtual?

Program visits and blood draws are in person at Lip Bunny Beauty in Fall River, MA. Some follow-up visits can be virtual.

Is the program open to men?

Yes. The Reset, Hoptimization, GLP-1, testing, injectables and IV therapy are all available to men. Bioidentical hormone therapy is offered for women only.

How do I pay?

A $200 deposit holds your spot and goes toward your program. The balance is due to start. Financing is available through Cherry and CareCredit. Our front desk can walk you through it.

What's included?

Your Bunny Baseline blood work at the start and graduation, Tiny Health gut tests, your customized supplement plan, ProLon fast kits, your visits and check-ins, a Styku body scan, the Heads Up app, and SweatEnergy. Add-on protocols, if recommended, are priced separately.

Day to Day

Living the Reset

I feel worse, not better. Is that normal?

In the first few days of a new step, mild tiredness, fogginess, a mild headache or digestion changes can be normal. Hydrate, make sure you're having daily bowel movements, and rest. Reach out if it's more than mild or lasts more than a few days. See quick help.

I slipped up. Did I ruin it?

No. Missing a day doesn't undo anything. Just restart the next meal or the next morning. Consistency over weeks is what counts, not perfection over days.

Can I still drink coffee?

Yes, in moderation. Have your lemon water with sea salt first, and add an extra glass of water for each cup. Keep it black or with a clean milk. On fast days, 1 to 2 cups of black coffee or plain tea are fine.

Can I drink alcohol?

We recommend taking a break, or at least moderating it. Alcohol adds to the load your liver is working to clear. No alcohol the day before a fast day or a blood draw.

How do I eat out?

Pick a protein and vegetables, ask for olive oil and lemon instead of creamy dressings, skip the fried sides, and use your bitters before a heavy meal. Then enjoy it. It's 80%, not 100%.

Should I exercise?

Yes. Daily walking first, then strength work. Use your included SweatEnergy subscription for workouts. Skip hard workouts on fast days and during a rough patch. Sweat 2 to 4 times a week if you tolerate it, and rehydrate with electrolytes.

Will the program affect my cycle?

Changes in stress, food, sleep and weight can shift your cycle a little. Note it in Heads Up and tell us at your next visit. Tell us right away if you might be pregnant.

Supplements & Medications

Supplements & meds

Do I stop my prescriptions?

No. Never stop a prescription without talking to us or the prescriber first. Keep fiber and binders at least 2 hours away from your medications.

When do my supplements start?

After your results review (program week 1). Before then, focus on your first habits: hydration, electrolytes and sleep.

What if I get pregnant during the program?

Stop your supplements and any GLP-1 or testosterone, and call us right away. Some herbs and medications aren't safe in pregnancy. We'll adjust your plan.

I have surgery or a dental procedure coming up.

Tell us as soon as it's scheduled. Some supplements need to be paused 1 to 2 weeks before a procedure.

My doctor started a new medication.

Message us with the name and dose. We'll check it against your supplements and your plan.
