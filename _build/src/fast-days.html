Safety First

Is fasting right for you?

Most patients do great. Tell us before your first fast if you:

  • Are pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding
  • Have diabetes or take blood sugar medication
  • Take blood pressure medication
  • Are underweight
  • Have a history of disordered eating
  • Have kidney or liver disease, or a heart condition
  • Have a nut or soy allergy (check your kit's label)
  • Are sick, or just started a new medication

We may adjust your plan, give you personal instructions, or skip fasting for you. That's okay. Your Reset still works.

Prep

The day before

  1. Eat a balanced dinner. Protein, vegetables and healthy fat. No heavy, greasy or late meal.
  2. No alcohol.
  3. Hydrate. Your usual glasses of water, plus one.
  4. Set out your kit so tomorrow is easy.
  5. Plan a calm day. Skip big events, hard workouts and long drives if you can.
The Day Of

Fast day rules

Eat & Drink

Only what's in your box

No extra snacks or meals. Water, herbal tea and lemon water with a pinch of sea salt any time. Sip all day.

1 to 2 cups of black coffee or plain tea are fine. No milk, cream or sugar. Add an extra glass of water for each.

Move Gently

Walk and stretch

Walking and stretching are perfect. Skip intense workouts, hot yoga and sauna today.

Expect

Some hunger is normal

Some hunger, low energy or a mild headache is normal. It passes. Feeling a little tired or cold is common. Feeling unwell is not.

Medications & supplements

Keep taking

Your prescription medications, unless your provider told you otherwise.

Pause for the day

All your supplements. Restart the next morning with breakfast.

Talk to us first

If you take diabetes or blood pressure medication, you'll get personal instructions before your first fast.

Hydration Is Key

Most fast-day headaches are thirst

  • Start your morning with lemon water with sea salt: 8 to 12 oz water, juice of half a lemon, a pinch of sea salt.
  • Sip, don't chug. A glass every hour or two. Aim for at least 8 glasses total. Herbal tea and lemon water count.
  • Another lemon water with sea salt in the afternoon if you feel tired or get a headache.
  • You're hydrated when your urine is pale yellow.

Tips that make it easier

Stay busy. A normal workday often goes faster than a free day.

Warm drinks help. Herbal tea takes the edge off hunger.

Get outside. A slow walk in fresh air resets your mood.

Go to bed early. Sleep is the easiest fasting hour. One day, then you're done.

Stop, eat and call us if
  • You feel dizzy, faint or lightheaded
  • You're shaky, sweaty or confused (signs of low blood sugar)
  • Your heart races or pounds
  • You're vomiting or can't keep fluids down

Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting? Call 911.

Ease Back In

The day after

  1. Break your fast gently. A light breakfast: eggs and vegetables, soup, or fruit with yogurt.
  2. Skip a big, heavy or sugary meal.
  3. Restart your supplements with breakfast.
  4. Note how you feel in your tracker or Heads Up.

Common Questions

Fast day questions

Can I have coffee?

Yes. 1 to 2 cups of black coffee or plain tea. No milk, cream or sugar. Add an extra glass of water for each cup.

Do I have to eat the foods in order?

Follow the order printed on your box as best you can. Spreading the food through the day keeps your energy steadier.

I'm really hungry. Can I eat something else?

Try warm herbal tea and lemon water with sea salt first. Hunger comes in waves and passes. If you feel dizzy, shaky or unwell, stop and eat. Your safety comes first.

Can I work on a fast day?

Yes. A normal workday often makes the day go faster. Avoid long drives, big events and hard workouts if you can.

Can I fast on my period?

Many women do fine. If your period makes you very tired or crampy, message us and we can move your fast a few days.

Do I take my medications and supplements?

Keep taking prescriptions unless we told you otherwise. Pause all your supplements and restart with breakfast the next morning.

Why did the scale drop?

A fast day can show a small drop. Most of it is water and returns in a day or two. The real changes show up in your labs, your energy and how you feel.

I broke my fast early. Did I ruin it?

No. You still gave your gut a rest. Note how you felt in your tracker and tell us at your next check-in. We'll plan for an easier next one.
