Welcome

Why this guide exists

Your body already knows how to heal. Every day it clears out what it doesn't need through your liver, gut, kidneys, lungs and skin. But modern life adds more than our bodies were built to handle. Over months and years, the load builds. That's when you start to feel tired, foggy, bloated, inflamed, or just "not yourself."

Your program is about turning that around. We lower the load, open the exits, and rebuild the soil your health grows from. This guide is your companion for every step.

How to use this guide

  1. Read this page first. It explains the "why" behind everything you'll do.
  2. Start your supplements after your results review, exactly as your label says.
  3. Pick one or two habits at a time. Not everything at once.
  4. Log how you feel in the Heads Up app.
  5. Bring your questions to every visit, so we can talk through what's working.

The Heart of the Bunny Method

Your gut is your soil

Picture a fruit tree. Give it rich soil, clean water, sunshine and good neighbors, and it grows strong. Put the same tree in poor soil and the leaves yellow, the fruit stays small, and pests move in. The tree isn't broken. Its soil is.

You are the same. Your gut is where you absorb nutrients, make key vitamins and brain chemicals, and house most of your immune system. When your soil is healthy, you "bear fruit": steady energy, clear skin, good sleep, a calm mood. When it isn't, you see the signs: bloating, fatigue, rashes, brain fog and getting sick often.

Toxin Load Over Time

The toxic bucket

Picture a bucket under a slowly dripping faucet. A few drops a day come from your water, skincare, food, the air and a stressful week. Your body has drains at the bottom: your liver, kidneys, gut, lungs and skin.

Problems start when the drips come in faster than the drains let them out, or when a drain gets clogged: a sluggish liver, constipation, poor sleep, chronic stress. The bucket rises slowly, over years. Then one day it overflows. That's when symptoms show up.

The good news: your drains respond fast when you support them.

Turn down the drips

Food, water, products, air.

Open the drains

Liver, gut, kidneys, skin, lungs.

Track how you feel

Energy, sleep, digestion, mood.

Habits and Mindset

You can change your body

Your body is not fixed. Your cells are always renewing. Your gut lining replaces itself every few days. Your gut bacteria shift within days of changing what you eat. Every day, you're building tomorrow's body out of today's choices.

1

Start tiny

Make it so easy you can't say no.

2

Stack it

Attach a new habit to one you already do. Take your supplements with your morning coffee.

3

Track it

What gets tracked gets done. Use the Heads Up app.

4

Expect bumps

Missing a day doesn't undo anything. Just restart the next day.

Your mindset is part of the protocol

How you think about your body shapes how you care for it. If you believe you're healing, every small win counts. Speak to your body like a plant you're helping grow. Patient. Kind. Consistent. Healing isn't a straight line, and every good choice counts.

Write it down

Three things I want to feel by graduation. Write them in your notes app or journal and read them on hard days.
