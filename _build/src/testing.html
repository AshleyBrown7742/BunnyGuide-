Blood draw guide

Your results are only as good as your prep. A few small steps help us read your labs clearly and build the right plan for you.

7 days

Biotin & collagen hold

Stop them a week before your draw.

7–10 AM

Best time to draw

Morning is best for hormones like testosterone and cortisol. Can't? Just note the time.

Fasting

Helpful, not required

Unless your order says "fasting required." If you ate, write down what and when.

24 to 48 hours before

  1. Skip intense workouts. A walk is perfect.
  2. No alcohol for at least 24 hours.
  3. Hydrate well the day before. Morning of, 1 to 2 glasses is plenty.
  4. Feeling sick or feverish? Reschedule.

Fasting draw?

  1. Stop eating 8 to 12 hours before.
  2. Water only, plenty of it. Save coffee for after.
  3. Bring a snack for right after your draw.

Couldn't fast? That's okay. Write down what you ate and when, and share it with us.

Special situations

Hormone creams or patches

Apply estradiol and testosterone creams 4 to 6 hours before your draw, same timing every time. Have blood drawn from the arm you did not apply cream to. Patches: apply as you normally would.

Oral progesterone

Take your usual bedtime dose the night before.

Thyroid medication

Take your usual dose about 4 hours before your draw, if possible. Changed your dose? Wait 6 weeks before rechecking.

Birth control pill

Estrogen, progesterone and SHBG look different on the pill. That's expected. We read them in that context.

GLP-1 / tirzepatide

No need to skip your injection. If fasting, eat soon after your draw.

Men: PSA prep

For 48 hours before, avoid ejaculation or sexual activity, cycling or spin, motorcycle or horseback riding, and heavy lifting. Tell us if you take finasteride or dutasteride, or had a recent prostate exam or procedure.

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your lab order
  • A current list of meds, supplements and herbs
  • Your cycle day, if you menstruate (Day 1 = first day of your period)

Draws are in-clinic at Lip Bunny Beauty, or at a remote Access location (additional draw fee).

Morning-of checklist

  • Biotin, collagen & creatine stopped 7 days ago
  • Iron held for 24 hours
  • Other supplements packed for after
  • Fasted, or noted what I ate and when
  • 1 to 2 glasses of water this morning
  • No alcohol or hard workout in 24 to 48 hrs
  • Thyroid med ~4 hrs before (if applicable)
  • Hormone cream 4 to 6 hrs before (if applicable)
  • Photo ID in hand

At-Home Kit · Tiny Health

Gut health test

A tiny swab with big insights. It maps the bacteria living in your gut: how well you digest, absorb and defend against inflammation. No spoons, no mess, about 5 minutes.

Before you collect

  • Antibiotics: wait 4 to 6 weeks after your last dose.
  • Probiotics: pause probiotic supplements for 3 to 5 days.
  • Food: eat like you normally do. We want a true picture.
  • Prescriptions: keep taking them.

How to collect

  1. Activate your kit first, using the QR code inside. When asked, choose your Lip Bunny provider.
  2. Wash your hands.
  3. After a bowel movement, wipe as usual with toilet paper.
  4. Twist open the tube and remove the swab. Don't touch the tip.
  5. Swab the stool on the tissue until you see a small smudge on the tip. A tiny bit is plenty.
  6. Return the swab to its tube and close it tightly.
  7. Seal the tube in the specimen bag.
  8. Place it in the prepaid mailer and drop it in any USPS mailbox, the same or next day.
What happens next

Results come to us in about 4 weeks. We'll review them with you. You don't need to interpret them yourself. Always follow the instructions inside your kit if they differ.

At-Home Kit · Tiny Health

Vaginal health test

Gentle, private and done in about 2 minutes. It maps the bacteria that protect your comfort, intimacy, fertility and hormone health.

Before you collect

  • 48 hours before: no sex and no lubricants. No douching, washes, or vaginal creams or suppositories.
  • Timing: not during your period. Wait until bleeding stops.
  • Medications: recent antibiotics or antifungals? Message us first. Keep taking prescriptions.
  • Symptoms? Unusual discharge, odor, itching or pelvic pain? Call us first. You may need a visit.

How to collect

  1. Activate your kit first. Choose your Lip Bunny provider.
  2. Wash your hands and get comfortable.
  3. Remove the swab. Don't touch the tip.
  4. With one hand, gently spread the skin outside the vagina.
  5. Insert just the tip of the swab into the vaginal opening.
  6. Rotate it for 10 to 15 seconds.
  7. Return the swab to its tube and close it tightly.
  8. Seal, place in the prepaid mailer, and drop in any USPS mailbox.
At-Home Kit · Access Labs

Saliva cortisol test

Cortisol should rise in the morning to wake you up and fall at night so you can sleep. Four saliva samples in one normal day show us your personal rhythm.

1

Morning

Within 30 minutes of waking, before breakfast, coffee or brushing.

2

Noon

Around 11 AM to 1 PM, before lunch.

3

Evening

Around 4 to 6 PM, before dinner.

4

Night

Around 10 PM to midnight, right before bed.

Write the exact time on each tube. Kit times differ? Follow the kit.

The day before

  • No alcohol for 24 hours
  • Skip licorice root and adaptogens (ashwagandha, rhodiola, holy basil) for 48 hours
  • Skip hydrocortisone creams

Collection day

  • Pick a normal day: not sick, traveling, or after a sleepless night
  • Skip caffeine if you can
  • No intense workouts. A walk is fine.
  • No lip balm or lipstick

Before each sample

  1. No food, gum, or brushing/flossing for 60 minutes
  2. Rinse with plain water 10 minutes before
  3. Then nothing in your mouth until you collect
Steroid medications

On prednisone, hydrocortisone, or a steroid inhaler or nasal spray? Tell us before testing. Don't stop them on your own. Work nights? Message us first to adjust your times.
