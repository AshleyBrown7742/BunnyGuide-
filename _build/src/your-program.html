Your Roadmap

What happens, and when

Our front desk books your next step before you leave every visit. All program visits are in person in Fall River. Some follow-ups can be virtual.

Getting started

Step 1

Website questionnaire

A short form so we understand your goals. Our front desk calls you to schedule a phone consultation.

Step 2

Intake form

Fill it out before your phone consult, so the call is about you, not paperwork.

Step 3

Phone consultation

We review your history, explain the program, and decide together if it's the right fit. A $200 deposit holds your spot and goes toward your program.

Step 4

Blood draw + live intake

Your Bunny Baseline blood work and a 20-minute conversation with your provider. You go home with your Tiny Health gut kit. See blood draw prep.

While you wait

Start your first habits

Results take a few weeks. You don't have to wait to feel better. Start with the habit priority list: hydration first.

Your program weeks

Week 1

Results review (70 minutes)

We walk through your labs and your gut test, then build your plan. Your Core 5 supplements start here, not before.

Every 4 weeks

ProLon fast days

One day with food, every 4 weeks, starting after your results review. 2 on the 8-week track, 3 on the 16-week track. See fast days.

Along the way

Check-ins

Weekly check-in texts and scheduled check-ins. Bring your tracker and your questions.

Week 8

Optimization checkpoint

If hormones or lean body mass are part of your goals, this is when we look at whether you're ready. 16-week patients also pick up their next 8 weeks of supplements and fast kits in person.

Near the end

Gut retest + graduation labs

Your Tiny Health retest is timed so results are back by graduation. Graduation blood work is drawn the week before.

Graduation

Before and after

We compare your labs, gut results and how you feel, and plan your next step. Celebrate.
