Step One

Get set up in Heads Up

Heads Up is your tracker for the whole program. Your care team sees what you log, so we can adjust your plan between visits.

  1. Watch for your invite. We send it by email when you join. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
  2. Download the Heads Up Health app. Search "Heads Up Health" in your phone's app store, then sign in with the email from your invite.
  3. Connect your devices if you have them, like a scale, watch or sleep tracker. This is optional.
  4. Log your first entry before your results review.
Can't find your invite?

Contact the office and we'll resend it.

Keep It Simple

What to log

A few minutes a day is plenty. Done is better than perfect.

Every day

Any symptoms, and your habits: water, movement, sunlight and supplements. You can also track your weight here if you like.

Once a week

Rate your energy, sleep, digestion, mood and bloating. Pick the same day each week so the trend is easy to read.

Gut and pH

Bowel movements per day (goal: at least 1). Morning pH if you are checking it.

Fast days

Log how you felt during and after each ProLon fast. See the ProLon Fast Guide.

Wins and questions

Note wins, food reactions and questions for your next visit. We review them with you.

Lab trends

When your labs are added, you can see your progress over time, from first labs to recheck.

Make It Stick

Tips for logging

  • Set a daily reminder on your phone, like right after your morning mineral water.
  • Missed a day? Just pick up again. No need to catch up.
  • Before each visit, open Heads Up and look at your week. Bring your questions.
Heads Up is not for emergencies

We don't watch it around the clock. No bowel movement for 2+ days, or reactions more than mild? Reach out. In an emergency, call 911.
