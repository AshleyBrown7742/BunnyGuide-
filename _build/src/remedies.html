Feeling Off?

Quick help by symptom

Gentle things to try at home. If a symptom is more than mild, lasts more than a few days, or worries you, message us or call {{PHONE}}. For what to eat, see foods for how you feel.

Constipation or no daily bowel movement

Daily bowel movements are a must during your Reset. Waste that sits too long can be reabsorbed.

  • Lemon water with sea salt first thing, then more water through the day
  • Take your fiber with a full glass of water
  • Walk after meals
  • Magnesium in the evening, as your label says
  • 2 prunes or dates a day

Reach outNo bowel movement for 2+ days, or belly pain, vomiting or fever.

Bloating or gas
  • Better Bitters spray 10 to 15 minutes before heavy meals (meat, dairy, foods that bloat you)
  • Hibiscus or ginger lemon tea after meals
  • Eat slowly and chew well. Stop at 80% full.
  • Introduce fiber slowly and drink more water with it
  • A short walk after eating

Bloating is common in the first weeks of a gut reset kit. Tell us at your check-in if it isn't settling.

Headache, tiredness or feeling "off" when starting something new

What's normal: in the first few days of a new step, you may feel a little more tired or foggy, a mild headache or achiness, a mild "flu-like" feeling, or changes in digestion. Your body is doing real work.

  • Hydrate: lemon water with sea salt, plus electrolytes
  • Make sure you're having a daily bowel movement
  • A detox bath and an early night
  • Walk gently. Skip hard workouts.

Reach outReactions more than mild, lasting more than a few days, a new rash or hives, belly pain, vomiting or fever.

Nausea
  • Ginger lemon tea, or a ginger supplement
  • Smaller meals, eaten slowly. Stop before you feel full.
  • Avoid greasy, fried and very sweet foods
  • Sip fluids between meals rather than with them

On a GLP-1? See GLP-1 side effect support. Call us if you can't keep fluids down.

Trouble sleeping
  • Magnesium in the evening, as your label says
  • A warm detox bath an hour before bed
  • No food 2 hours before bed, no screens the last hour
  • Morning sunlight to set your body clock
  • Calming music or a frequency track as you fall asleep

Tell us if a new supplement seems to make you wired or anxious. Some B vitamins can, and we can adjust.

Stressed or overwhelmed
  • Slow breathing: in for 4, out for 6, for 3 minutes
  • Get outside. Barefoot on grass if you can.
  • A gratitude meditation, morning or night
  • Pick one habit this week, not five

Ritual

The Bunny Bath

A warm bath supports your skin, your largest exit route, and helps you wind down.

Recipe

Add to a warm bath

  • 2 cups Epsom salt
  • 2 cups sea salt
  • 1 cup baking soda

Soak 20 minutes. Rinse off, rehydrate with water and electrolytes, and moisturize with a clean lotion or oil.

Why it works

Epsom salt (magnesium) relaxes muscles and calms the nervous system. Sea salt adds minerals. Baking soda softens and soothes skin.

Make it a ritual

Dim the lights, put on calming music or a frequency track, and leave your phone in another room.

Skip or ask first

Skip hot baths if you're pregnant. Check with us first if you have a heart condition or low blood pressure.

Teas & tonics

Dandelion root

Traditionally used for liver and bile flow. One cup daily. Skip if you have kidney disease.

Milk thistle

Gentle liver support. One cup daily.

Ginger lemon

Warming. Great after meals and for nausea.

Hibiscus

Tart, antioxidant-rich and caffeine-free. Hot or iced. Great on your fast day. Skip if pregnant. Ask us first if you take blood pressure or diabetes medication.

The Bunny Method Chai

Simmer 2 cups water with a pinch each of cloves, Ceylon cinnamon, nutmeg, cardamom, a little cayenne, fresh ginger, turmeric and a crack of black pepper for 10 minutes. Strain and add warm milk of choice. The black pepper helps you absorb the turmeric.

What Escorts Waste Out

Binders

Once your liver and gut release waste, something needs to grab it in the gut so it leaves in your stool instead of being reabsorbed. That's the job of a binder.

Your everyday binder

Your GLP-1 Daily Support Fiber binds waste, feeds your good bacteria and keeps you regular. It's part of your Core 5.

Stronger binders, only when we prescribe them

BinderWhen we use it
Modified citrus pectin (like PectaClear)Spike protein and heavy metal add-on protocols
Activated charcoalOccasionally, for a rough day (headache, fatigue)
ZeoliteRegular (non-nano) zeolite only. Nano particles are small enough to cross the gut lining.
Timing rule

Keep binders at least 2 hours away from food, medications and other supplements. Binders can grab those too.

A Daily Mirror

Your pH tracking

Your urine pH is a simple daily mirror of how your food and habits are balancing out. Use the pH strips in your welcome bag on your first-morning urine.

Healthy range

First-morning urine is generally 6.5 to 7.5. Don't stress over one reading. Look at the weekly trend.

Why terrain matters

Unwanted organisms tend to thrive in an acidic, low-oxygen environment, like weeds in poor soil. Alkaline foods and herbs help change the terrain.

Log your readings in the notes of your weekly tracker.
