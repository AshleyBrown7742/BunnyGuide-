Habit 1

Morning mineral water

Soil can't grow anything without water, and neither can you. But plain water alone isn't the goal. Too much plain water can dilute your sodium and electrolytes.

Try This When You Wake

Load your first glass

Your lymphatic system works through the night. A big glass of mineral-rich water first thing helps your body flush out what it cleared while you slept.

  1. Upon waking: a large, full glass of filtered water.
  2. Load it with electrolytes and trace minerals. Easy version: juice of half a lemon and a pinch of sea salt.
  3. Then tea or coffee.

Coffee tip: waiting 60 to 90 minutes after waking is best. But nobody is perfect.

Habit 2

Electrolytes

Electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) help your body actually use the water you drink. They matter most on workout days, sauna days, hot days, and fast days.

  • Your morning lemon water with sea salt is your first electrolyte of the day.
  • Add an electrolyte drink around workouts and after sauna or a hot bath.
  • Choose ones without added sugar, artificial sweeteners or dyes.
  • Mineral-rich foods count too: leafy greens, avocado, bananas, nuts and seeds.
Check with us first

If you have kidney disease, heart failure, or take blood pressure medication or a "water pill," ask before adding salt or electrolyte products.

Habit 3

Sleep

7 to 9 hours nightly. Your brain's "wash cycle" runs almost entirely during deep sleep. Skipping sleep is like clogging the drain.

Morning and sunset light set your body clock. See your sunlight directions.

Your wind-down checklist

  • No food 2 hours before bed
  • Dim the lights after dinner
  • Screens away for the last hour
  • 10 pages of a real book
  • Cool, dark, quiet bedroom
  • Same bedtime most nights, weekends too
Habit 4

Stress and your nervous system

Digestion, elimination and repair all run in "rest and digest" mode. When you're stuck in fight-or-flight, your body pulls energy away from exactly the pathways this program supports. Calming your nervous system is part of the protocol.

Gratitude practice

A few minutes of guided gratitude meditation, morning or night. Free guided sessions are easy to find on YouTube.

Sound and frequency

Calming music or frequency tracks during a bath, a walk or before sleep. Find sounds by symptom.

Nature

Time outside, barefoot on grass or sand, near trees or water. The original reset.

Movement and yoga

A slow flow or a walk both shift you out of stress mode.

Energy work and Reiki

Many people find it deeply calming. Ask us about options.

Therapy

A trained outside perspective is one of the most effective nervous system tools there is.

Breathing

Slow breaths with a longer exhale (in for 4, out for 6) for a few minutes tell your body it's safe. Or try box breathing below.

Fewer screens

Especially in the hour before bed. Less scrolling, less comparison.

Neuromodulation

Vagus nerve tools are worth exploring with us if stress feels hard to shift.

Try It Now

Box breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4. Follow the dot around the box. Great before bed, before a hard conversation, or any time you feel wired.

Feel dizzy or lightheaded? Stop and breathe normally.

Habit 5

Movement, sun and sweat

A plant needs sunlight, wind and water moving through it. So do you.

Walk daily

Even 20 minutes moves your lymph: your body's waste highway, which has no pump except you moving. Walk in nature when you can.

Then build muscle

Once walking is a habit, add strength work 2 to 3 times a week. Muscle is your metabolism's best friend.

Work out with SweatEnergy

Your SweatEnergy subscription is included in your program. Use it for your strength workouts at your own pace. We activate it with you. About your tools.

Sweat

Sauna or a good sweat 2 to 4 times a week, as tolerated, 15 to 20 minutes. Rehydrate with electrolytes afterward. Skip sauna on fast days.

Sunlight, 3 times a day

Morning, midday and sunset. Barefoot outside is even better. See your sunlight directions.

Dry brushing

Before a shower, brush skin with gentle strokes toward your heart. Supports skin and lymph flow.

Listen to your energy

Low-energy day? A walk or a gentle SweatEnergy session counts. Don't push hard workouts during a rough patch or on a fast day.

The Bunny Method Tip

Pick one of these to focus on this month. Trying to fix all of them at once usually means fixing none.

Your Daily Sunshine

Sunlight, three times a day

Plants run on sunlight. So do you. Light sets your body clock, and a well-set clock supports sleep, mood, hormones and every detox pathway.

Morning
5–10 min

Go outside and face the sun

Let the morning light reach your eyes, as soon as you can after waking. Even 2 minutes is great.

Midday
20+ min

Direct midday sunlight

At least 20 minutes outside in direct sun. A walk at lunch counts.

Sunset
5–10 min

Watch the sun go down

Sunset light tells your body the day is ending, so you're ready to sleep.

Even better

Go barefoot on grass, sand or soil. Skip sunglasses for these short sessions if your eyes are comfortable. Windows block much of the light, so step outside.

Protect your eyes and skin

Face toward the sun, but never stare directly at it. Build up midday time slowly and don't let your skin burn. Ask us first if you burn easily, have a history of skin cancer, or take a medication that makes you sun-sensitive.

Habit 6

Diet, one change at a time

Food is your biggest lever, but it's last on the list for a reason. Once your hydration, sleep, stress and movement are steady, food changes stick. Add these one at a time:

  1. No fast food.
  2. Moderate processed sugar and refined carbs. Trade them for a little raw honey or fruit. Your taste buds reset in about two weeks.
  3. Moderate alcohol.
  4. No food 2 hours before bed.
  5. Lemon water with sea salt first thing, every morning.
