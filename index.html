Your Daily Rhythm

A simple day on the Reset

Not a rulebook. A rhythm to come back to when the week gets busy.

Morning

Wake & hydrate

A full glass of water with electrolytes and trace minerals. Then 5 to 10 minutes of morning sun outside.

Meals

Feed the soil

Supplements as your label says. Bitters before heavy meals. Plants on most of your plate.

Afternoon

Move

A 20-minute walk keeps your lymph moving. Outside when you can.

Evening

Wind down

No food 2 hours before bed. Dim lights, fewer screens, 7 to 9 hours of sleep.

Not feeling right?

Mild tiredness, a mild headache or a change in digestion can be normal for a few days when you start something new. Reach out if it's more than mild, lasts more than a few days, or you haven't had a bowel movement in 2+ days.
