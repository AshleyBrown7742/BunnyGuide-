Keep It Safe

Supplement rules

1

Your label is the boss

Your dose and timing are on your label and your plan. Follow those over anything general you read here or online.

2

One new thing at a time

Start one new supplement every couple of days, unless we tell you otherwise. If something doesn't agree with you, you'll know which one.

3

Fiber and binders: 2 hours from meds

Take fiber and binders at least 2 hours away from prescriptions, including thyroid medication and birth control, so they don't block absorption.

4

Tell us about every medication

Including new prescriptions, over-the-counter meds and herbs. Some supplements interact with blood thinners, blood pressure, thyroid, diabetes and mood medications.

5

Pregnancy changes everything

Some herbs aren't safe in pregnancy or breastfeeding. Tell us right away if you're pregnant or trying to conceive.

6

Pause before labs and surgery

Some supplements need to stop before a blood draw or procedure. See the supplement hold guide and tell us about any upcoming procedure.

Common Questions

Supplement questions

I forgot my supplements. Should I double up?

No. Just take your next dose at the usual time. Missing a day doesn't undo anything.

Do I take supplements on my fast day?

No. Pause all your supplements on your ProLon fast day and restart with breakfast the next morning. Keep taking prescription medications unless we told you otherwise.

Can I keep taking my own supplements?

Bring everything you take to your results review. Some may overlap with your plan, and some may interact. We'll tell you what to keep, pause or stop.

A supplement is upsetting my stomach.

Try taking it with food (unless the label says empty stomach). If it doesn't settle in a few days, pause that one supplement and message us.

I have trouble swallowing pills.

Let us know. Many supplements come as powders, liquids or smaller capsules, and some capsules can be opened into food. Ask before opening any capsule.
