Call us if you notice

  • Reactions that feel more than mild or last more than a few days
  • No bowel movement for 2+ days
  • A new rash or hives
  • Belly pain, vomiting or fever
  • Nausea that won't settle, or you can't keep fluids down
  • You might be pregnant
(508) 388-2200

Call 911 for

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing
  • Fainting
  • Swelling of the face or throat
  • Sudden face drooping, weakness, confusion, or trouble speaking or seeing
  • Pain, swelling or warmth in one leg
  • Severe belly pain that spreads to your back
  • Black or bloody stools
